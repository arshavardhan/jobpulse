Help & Knowledge Base

Support & Frequently Asked Questions

How are jobs verified?

Every job is ingested directly from the employer's official career page or public ATS API. Our engine executes automated reachability checks so expired 404 links are rejected or retired.

What does "Direct ATS" mean?

When multiple boards list the same role, JobPulse upgrades the application link to the employer's primary direct ATS. You apply directly into their hiring funnel.

How do I report an expired listing?

Open any job card to view its details and click "Report this listing". Select the reason (Broken link, Expired, Duplicate) and our automated lifecycle engine will re-verify or expire it immediately.

Why are LinkedIn/Indeed "Authorization Required"?

We strictly respect terms of service and robots.txt. Closed commercial platforms require official client partner API credentials; JobPulse never deploys automated bypass scripts.
